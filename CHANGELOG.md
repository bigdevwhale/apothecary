## [1.0.0]

The first version.

- Four places: the cool cellar (fridge), the pantry, the ice chest (freezer) and the cabinet (medicine box).
- The deadline is the package date or "opened + the days it keeps open", whichever is first; the ice chest restarts the clock. A guide of about 170 products fills it all in.
- Cooking is brewing: 40 recipes from the mix of essences, two rare ones, six customers asking for what is about to be lost, coins for twelve things in the shop, guild ranks by brews and loss.
- The calendar, the ledger with eight weeks in bars, the night audit and the morning bell.
- The medicine box: the guild's kit, warnings a month ahead, vitamin courses, expired medicines to a pharmacy.
